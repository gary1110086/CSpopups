# NASA historical image collection

These twelve images were retrieved from the NASA Image and Video Library on 23 October 2026 for an educational, non-commercial wallpaper search exercise. NASA is not affiliated with or endorsing this project. These are historical photographs, not AI-generated images. No NASA logos or identifiable portraits were selected.

Usage guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The gallery links each original record and original download. Delivered JPEGs preserve the original aspect ratio, resized inside3200×2400 and re-encoded at quality91. WebP thumbnails are cropped for catalogue display. Wallpaper exports can add letterboxing or crop as explicitly selected. The original data, date and credits below are retained; our short gallery descriptions are original summaries.
