# Cooking and outing expansion — original assets and source notes

Revision: 23 October 2026. No third-party article text, venue photography, logo or review was copied. The 16 added recipes and 16 added outing articles are original bilingual writing. Ingredient budgets are illustrative estimates, not live shop prices. Outing clock times are suggested itinerary times, not venue hours. Current access, actual admission and booking terms are linked to operators where applicable.

## Image provenance

Five images were made with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this project. The four contact sheets have exactly four columns and two rows, 2172 × 724 pixels. WebP copies are quality-88 delivery encodings of the originals. Background positioning selects a proportional 3:2 cell; no image is stretched across a different aspect ratio. The standalone pewter illustration is delivered at up to 1200 pixels wide.

These are **original illustrative images**, not documentary photographs of the named venues. The outing details repeat that distinction. The ceramics cell in outing-deep-2 is retained in its source sheet but is not used for the pewter article; that article uses the separately generated pewter image.

## Official factual references checked

The article prose and suggested itineraries are original. These references verify venue identity, basic visitor context or the specifically stated rules; they do not imply that an operator offers our example itinerary, quoted allowance or suggested clock time.

- [Muzium Negara visitor information](https://www.muziumnegara.gov.my/index.php/en/visiting-info): public transport; gallery photography without flash, special filming permission; no food/drinks in galleries.
- [Suria KLCC — KLCC Park](https://www.suriaklcc.com.my/attractions/klcc-park/): venue identity and park context.
- [Tourism Malaysia — Kuala Lumpur destinations](https://www.malaysia.travel/explore/top-places-to-visit-in-kuala-lumpur): Central Market and heritage-area context.
- [Tourism Malaysia — museums and galleries](https://www.malaysia.travel/explore/must-visit-museums-and-galleries-in-kl): named museum/gallery context.
- [Books Kinokuniya — in-store pickup](https://kinokuniya.com.my/pages/in-store-pickup): Suria KLCC store identity.
- [Taman Tugu — things to do](https://tamantugu.my/things-to-do/): separate 1.0 km Blue Trail and free trail entry, subject to current access.
- [NU Sentral — contact and transport](https://www.nusentral.com/contact/): address and KL Sentral transport-hub access.
- [MBPJ — Taman Jaya](https://www.mbpj.gov.my/ms/pelawat/rekreasi/taman-jaya): public park, lake and nearby Taman Jaya LRT.
- [Pusat Sains Negara — visitors](https://psn.gov.my/pelawat/): official current visitor-information starting point.
- [Perbadanan Putrajaya — Anjung Floria](https://tourism.ppj.gov.my/destination/detail/23): named waterfront recreation area.
- [Royal Selangor Visitor Centre](https://visitorcentre.royalselangor.com/): museum, live craftsmanship, pewter-dish School of Hard Knocks workshop; adult supervision for under-12s. Our RM100 is a comparison allowance, not a quoted workshop price.

No real bookings or payments are collected by these articles. Operator and map links open normal browser tabs. Suggested walking distances are editorial estimates for the selected short route, except the explicitly sourced Taman Tugu 1.0 km Blue Trail.
