# Real media source register — Browse Deep2

Retrieved 24 October 2026. These are downloaded, individually matched source photographs and historical film images. No generated contact sheet supplies the current Browse movie, wallpaper, recipe or outing catalogue imagery. The independent original `/wallpapers/` vector prototype is unchanged.

The twelve former Browse artworks were replaced while retaining their route IDs. Twelve fictional film entries were replaced by real silent-era films; the twelve existing Blender IDs and three playable local videos are preserved. Recipe text, quantities, venue/task constraints and application behaviour are unchanged.

## Reuse and changes

- Attribution belongs to the named photographers/artists, not this project. Every item links its source record and licence.
- Each full JPEG and WebP thumbnail is a proportional resize/re-encoding of the cited original. Catalogue/detail containers may crop only the on-screen view. Individual photo adaptations retain the source licence, including the corresponding CC BY-SA version. The website code and original editorial copy are separate.
- File dimensions below describe the actual locally delivered image; original source dimensions are separately recorded. Larger desktop exports are resampled and do not create native detail.
- Recipe photographs are serving or ingredient references, not a claim that the photographer cooked our exact recipe. Explicit differences are stated in the visible caption. Venue photographs show the recorded location/date; neighbourhood references are labelled, with no claim of current conditions or partnership.
- Historical film images are public-domain records; this does not confer rights to modern restorations, colourisations, musical scores, logos or distributor services. Only the source image is copied. A Trip to the Moon uses Méliès’s preliminary poster drawing, and The Great Train Robbery / Nosferatu use actual film stills.

Existing Blender stills: [OPEN-FILM-STILLS.md](OPEN-FILM-STILLS.md). Existing playable excerpts and trailer: [VIDEO-SOURCES.md](VIDEO-SOURCES.md). Historical NASA wallpapers: [NASA-SOURCES.md](NASA-SOURCES.md).
