# Field Guide and Trackside — sources and media provenance

Source and license checks: 2026-10-24. The two sites are independent browsing prototypes, not affiliated with Cornell Lab, eBird, Rapid KL, MRT Corp or Wikimedia.

## Category and information references

- [Cornell Lab — All About Birds](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/): species-account structure, field marks, size, habitat and behaviour. Species text in this project is an original short paraphrase, not a copied account. Each record links its primary identification page below and in the public UI. No Cornell or Macaulay Library photography is copied.
- [eBird Explore](https://ebird.org/explore): species-first exploration and a separate personal list informed the Field Guide information architecture. No eBird checklist data or branding is copied.
- [Rapid KL](https://myrapid.com.my/): operator-style journey-planner layout, real line and station naming. Direct official map retrieval returned HTTP403 during research, so no map artwork was copied. The central line order and connections were cross-checked with MRT information and the official indexed service pages. Trackside's graph is a deliberately limited central-corridor selection, not the full Klang Valley network.
- [MRT Corp — travel with MRT](https://www.mymrt.com.my/public/travel-with-mrt/): Kajang-line station sequence and interchange context. Actual services and access must be checked with the operator.
- [Cornell — American Crow identification](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/american_crow/id): current account includes Pacific Northwest birds formerly separated as Northwestern Crow. The crow photo retains its historical filename and original attribution.

## Content and timetable boundaries

Field Guide contains 18 actual North American species, with short original English/Chinese identification summaries. Colours are possible plumage colours, not a claim that every sex/age/season matches the same photograph. Lengths are species length ranges, not standing height. Chinese names aid reading; scientific names identify taxa. Size groups are approximate identification aids and overlap.

Trackside models 28 station-platform nodes on three real central Kuala Lumpur corridors and five pedestrian connections. Separate same-name platforms retain separate IDs. The Kelana Jaya corridor runs KL Sentral–Jelatek, the Kajang corridor Muzium Negara–Maluri, and the Monorail corridor KL Sentral–Titiwangsa. The first two boundaries are study limits, not actual line termini. Full networks and other real interchanges are outside the model.

All train intervals (KJ6/KG8/MR10 minutes), modeled boundary departure windows (06:00–23:30), ride durations, transfer walks and entrance/exit allowances are original demonstration data. Intermediate departure phase includes cumulative ride time in the selected direction. Selected dates do not change the illustrative daily schedule; dates are retained for calendar export. Routes use a time-dependent graph with actual computed waiting, ride, transfer and access components. The public pages disclose these boundaries and do not claim live predictions or official fares. No ticket, payment or credential endpoint exists.

Calendar files use Asia/Kuala_Lumpur (UTC+8), tentative event status, real start/end times from the chosen model result and all leg details. Sighting notes and saved journeys remain in the seed's local session state. TXT/ICS files are generated locally.

## Media processing and licenses

All images listed below were fetched from their original Wikimedia Commons files after checking the Commons imageinfo extmetadata author, license, license URL and file-description page. Full-sized local WebP copies were auto-oriented, fit inside 1200×1000 without enlargement and encoded at quality86. Separate thumbnails fit inside440×360 at quality82. Site card framing may crop thumbnails; species detail photographs use contain to preserve field marks. These are real photographs, not AI images. Original image descriptions are retained in journey-media.js to make the depicted subject auditable.

Each resized/re-encoded derivative is offered under the same license/version as its source, with credit and source/license links in the detail or sources UI. The original authors retain rights. CC BY-SA versions are not collapsed into one generic license. Audio MP3s are unmodified matching field recordings; source descriptions and overlap notes are retained. Source anchors may open remote pages, but all runtime images and audio load locally.

## Photo context cautions

- The KL Sentral photograph depicts the **Monorail entrance**, not the Kelana Jaya station building.
- The KLCC image depicts the LRT station concourse and fare gates. Its Commons filename uses the word platform; the visible scene is labeled concourse in this prototype.
- The Kelana Jaya carriage interior is a general context photograph and is explicitly labeled when used on another station page. It is not presented as a photograph of that station.
- Cardinal has one verified photo. Other species have two; the discarded second cardinal candidate depicted a different species and is not shipped.
- Primary Ruby-throated Hummingbird photograph shows an adult male, while the second photograph has a different age/plumage. Field marks explain that the gorget can look dark with angle and is absent in females/young.
- Chickadee audio contains other birds too; the UI identifies approximate chickadee call times00:00,00:28,00:32. No unrelated audio is used on the other16species.
