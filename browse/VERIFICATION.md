## Practical limits

Chrome is the tested browser; other browser families and extensions were not exhaustively tested. Native tab policies may block a requested tab, in which case the explicit fallback remains available. Notes and saved choices are local to the browser tab and do not synchronize between computers. Rail timings are an illustrative model, and outing budgets/routes are planning examples rather than live transport or venue data. Bird records explicitly cover North America. Camera sellers/listings and retail product specifications are teaching fiction; no enquiry, credential, permission or payment is submitted. Three film previews have original audio; captions are not supplied. Real food/location images carry subject qualifications rather than implying an exact cooked result or current site conditions.
