## Fixes found during integration

- A recipe servings redraw could remove an assignment notebook placed inside the scene root. The notebook now lives outside that redraw region; modal inertness covers both regions.
- Newly redrawn advertiser anchors and nativeGETforms require link/seed/task reconciliation. A child-list observer now wires the new elements without replacing scene event handlers.
- A form control namedmethod can shadowform.method. Method detection usesgetAttribute.
- Coupon visual and headline both advertiseRM100.
- Video seeking requiresHTTPbyte ranges. The packaged dependency-free previewserver returns206 andcorrectContent-Range; play/seek was verified usingRange-capableservers.

## Limits

Chrome is the tested browser. Native tab policies/extensions may block a requestednewtab; a visiblefallback remains. Other browser families and installed extensions were not exhaustively tested. Local task state does not synchronize across computers. Film excerpts have original sound but no captions. Outing information and recipe budgets are planning examples, not live inventory or prices. Generated outing images are illustrations.
